Store Bot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Operated by Yazan Alsharif

What this is

Store Bot is a messaging automation that replies to customer messages sent via Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook to an online clothing store's business accounts (including techno.stores_jo). It answers product questions and helps customers place orders.

Information we collect

  • Messaging identifiers — the sender ID or phone number tied to your Instagram, WhatsApp, or Facebook conversation.
  • Message content — the text you send to the store's account, so the automation can respond.
  • Order details you provide — name, phone number, delivery area, and the items you order, if you choose to place an order through the chat.

We do not collect this information through any other means, and we do not access your broader Instagram, WhatsApp, or Facebook profile beyond what the platform shares for an active conversation.

How we use it

  • To generate and send automated replies to your messages.
  • To record and fulfill orders you place, including handing delivery details to our delivery partner.
  • To keep track of an ongoing conversation so replies stay relevant to what you already said.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Storage and retention

Conversation and order data is stored in a private database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) accessible only to the store's operators. We keep it only as long as needed to fulfill an order and handle related support, after which it may be deleted.

Sharing

We share order details (name, phone, address, items) only with our delivery partner, solely to deliver what you ordered. We do not share your data with any other third party, and we never sell it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us below.
